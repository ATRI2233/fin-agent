==============================================

## Changes Made
Refactored `main/framework/core/workflow_engine.py` from 562 lines to 242 lines (57% reduction).

### What was removed:
- `_execute_in_order()` — moved to WorkflowService._execute_in_order()
- `_wait_for_predecessors()` — moved to WorkflowService
- `_compute_level()` — moved to WorkflowService
- `_execute_single_node()` — moved to WorkflowService._execute_wrapped()
- `_build_prompt()` — moved to individual node executors (debate_executor._build_enriched_prompt, agent_executor)
- The 4-type if/elif block in execute_node() — replaced by NodeExecutorRegistry dispatch

### What was added:
- `_get_service()` — lazy WorkflowService factory with protocol adapters
- `_sync_from_service()` — copies service state back for collect_results() compat
- `_WorkflowRepoAdapter` — wraps db session to satisfy WorkflowRepositoryProtocol
- `_ExecServiceAdapter` — wraps db session to satisfy ExecutionServiceProtocol (create_execution_for_workflow, mark_downstream_skipped)

### What was kept (backward compat):
- Same constructor signature: `(workflow_id, params, dispatcher, db, status_callback, execution_id, exec_repo, workflow_repo)`
- `execute()` — delegates to service.run(), accepts optional args with fallback to constructor defaults
- `execute_node()` — 4 lines: registry lookup + delegate to service
- `handle_failure()` — delegates to service.handle_failure()
- `collect_results()` — kept as-is (synced from service state)
- `_cleanup_sessions()` — kept as thin wrapper
- `_build_predecessors()`, `_find_downstream()`, `_is_leaf()`, `_is_only_successor()` — delegate to workflow_graph module
- `_get_agent_name()`, `_find_node()` — kept as-is
- WorkflowRetryHandler import (still needed)
